Delete your PakChat account

In the app (the quickest way)

Open PakChat on your phone, then go to Settings › Account › Delete my account, and type your phone number.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your name, photo, about, phone number and Gmail.
  • Your chat backup on our server, your devices and their keys.
  • You leave all your groups and communities.
  • Messages and files still waiting to be delivered to you.

Messages you already sent stay on the phones of the people who got them, as with any messaging app. Photos and files you already sent stay for them until their usual 30 days are up, and a group photo you set stays with the group. Our nightly safety copies of the server are deleted within 14 days.
