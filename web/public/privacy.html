PakChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version.
  • Your messages, calls, photos, videos, voice notes, documents and backups are locked end to end. Only you and the people you talk to can open them. We can't, and nobody we work with can.
  • We keep only what PakChat needs to work: your account, who is in which chat, and locked messages until they're delivered (at most 30 days).
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything we hold about you at any time.

1. Who we are

PakChat is a messaging app for Android and the web (PakChat Web), made by an independent developer in Pakistan. In this policy, "PakChat", "we" and "us" mean PakChat and the person who runs it, and "you" means anyone who uses the app or the website. This policy covers the PakChat app, PakChat Web, and the PakChat website.

2. What we can't see

PakChat uses the Signal Protocol for end-to-end encryption, the same method used by Signal and WhatsApp. Everything below is locked on your device before it leaves, and only unlocked on the devices of the people you sent it to:

  • the words of your messages, in chats with one person, in groups and in communities;
  • photos, videos, voice notes, documents, stickers, GIFs and locations you send;
  • voice and video calls, including screen sharing;
  • status updates;
  • link preview cards (your own phone makes these, see section 4);
  • chat backups you save to our server, which are locked with a password only you know.

The keys that unlock these never leave your devices. Our server only passes the locked copies along, so we couldn't read them even if we were asked to.

3. What we collect and why

Your account

  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's email address, name and Google account ID. Google confirms these to us; we never see your Google password.
  • If you add or sign in with a phone number: your phone number. To prove the number is yours, you send a short code from WhatsApp to PakChat's WhatsApp number (see section 4), or we send you a code by SMS. We keep the number on your account; the codes are deleted within a day.
  • Your profile: the name, "about" text and profile photo you choose, and your privacy settings.

We use these to create your account, sign you in, and let people who already know your number or email find you on PakChat.

Finding your contacts on PakChat

If you allow PakChat to read your phone's contacts, your phone scrambles each phone number and email address (a one-way "hash") before sending them, and our server answers which of them use PakChat. We don't store the list your phone sends, and your contacts' names never leave your phone.

Your devices

  • The public encryption keys of each phone or browser you use PakChat on (the private halves stay on the device), so others can start locked chats with you.
  • A device name (like "Phone" or the browser's name) and when each device was last active, so you can see and remove your linked devices.
  • A push token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, so we can wake your phone when something arrives (see section 4).

Chats, groups and communities

  • Which chats, groups and communities you're in, and who is an admin.
  • Group and community names, descriptions, photos, settings and invite links. These are not end-to-end locked, because the server needs them to show invites and manage members.
  • For each message waiting to be delivered: who sent it, which chat, its number in the chat, and when. This is how the server knows where to pass it. The message itself stays locked.

Online and last seen

Whether you're online now, and when you were last active, so we can show it to the people your privacy settings allow (everyone, your contacts, or nobody).

Reports

If you report a person, group or community, your report includes the reason you choose and the last few messages from that chat, which your own phone unlocks and sends with the report. We use this only to review the report and keep PakChat safe.

Technical information

Like any internet service, our server sees the IP address your device connects from. We use it briefly to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts) and to keep the service secure. Our server keeps short technical logs (such as which kind of request was made and when, and errors) to fix problems; these logs don't contain message contents.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location (unless you send it in a locked message), your browsing, your contacts' names, or anything for advertising. We don't use analytics or tracking tools in the app or on the website.

5. What other people can see

  • Your name, about text and profile photo, and your online and last seen, to the people your privacy settings allow. Profile photos are protected in transit and shown only to those people, but like WhatsApp's they aren't end-to-end locked.
  • Your phone number or email, to people you chat with and people who already have it.
  • In groups and communities: your name, photo and messages, to other members. Read receipts (blue ticks), if you leave them on.
  • Anything you send can be saved, copied or forwarded by the people who receive it.

7. Your choices

  • Privacy settings: choose who sees your last seen, online, profile photo, about and groups, and who can add you to groups.
  • Read receipts and link previews: turn them off in Settings.
  • Block anyone, and report people or chats.
  • Contacts: you can say no to, or later remove, PakChat's access to your contacts in your phone's settings.
  • Linked devices: see every phone and browser on your account and log any of them out.
  • Your rights: you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us (section 14). We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

To delete your PakChat account, email us from the Google account or with the phone number you use on PakChat, and say you want your account deleted (section 14). An option to do it yourself in the app (Settings › Account › Delete my account) is coming.

When your account is deleted, we delete your profile, phone number, email, devices and keys, your backup, your memberships in chats, and any messages and files still waiting for you. Locked messages you already sent stay on the phones of the people who received them, as with any messaging app. Copies in our nightly safety copies disappear within 14 days.

9. Security

Besides end-to-end encryption, all connections to PakChat use HTTPS (TLS). Session tokens and sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes. Our server runs with automatic security updates and a firewall. No system is perfectly secure, but we design PakChat so that even if our server were broken into, your messages, calls and files would stay locked.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use PakChat, or older if the law where you live requires it. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Where your information is

Our server is hosted by Oracle Cloud in Mumbai, India. Google and Meta may handle the information they receive (section 4) in other countries under their own policies. By using PakChat, you understand your information is handled in these places, always protected as this policy describes.

12. Law enforcement and safety

We may share the limited information we hold (section 3) if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. Because messages, calls and files are end-to-end locked, we can't provide their contents to anyone.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
